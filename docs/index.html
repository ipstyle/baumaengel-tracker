Baumängel Tracker In App Review

Snag lists for iPhone — rooms, defects with photo and deadline, tick them off, and hand over a clean PDF report. Everything stays on the device.

Free Open source · MIT No account No network access Export & import
Source on GitHub Support & bug reports

What it looks like

Screens from the app, in dark appearance.

What it does

Projects and rooms

  • One object per project, with address and progress
  • Rooms in your own order, standard rooms in one tap

Defects

  • Title, note and optional deadline
  • Open or fixed, with the date it was fixed
  • Overdue deadlines are marked

Photos

  • Camera or photo picker, several per defect
  • Full screen, deletable one by one

PDF report

  • A4 with header, summary and page numbers
  • One section per room, one block per defect

Your data, your control

What you record belongs to you, and you can get all of it out at any time. A backup writes every project, room, defect and photo into a single file that you can put wherever you like. On import you decide whether it is added to your data or replaces it. No account, no cloud, no lock-in: changing device means importing the backup and carrying on.

Requirements and permissions

iPhone, iOS 17 or later, portrait. The app is in German.

For whatPermission
Taking a photo Camera — optional; the photo picker works without it
Choosing an image System photo picker, no access to the whole library

Privacy

The app collects nothing and makes no network connections. Projects, rooms, defects, notes and photos stay in the app's protected storage on the device. A report leaves the device only when you share it yourself. Full text: PRIVACY.md

Build it yourself

The Xcode project is generated from project.yml: brew install xcodegen · xcodegen generate. Source: github.com/ipstyle/baumaengel-tracker
